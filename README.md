# World news summary — make.fun entry (news-summary-01)

One summary, in English, of the world's news of Wednesday 11 November 2026.
The program copies `summary.txt` to `/output/entry.txt`; an AI judge reads
two summaries at a time and picks the one a reader who missed the day would
most want to read.

The summary covers 10 stories, ordered by importance, each with its source
named: the second day of US-brokered Russia-Ukraine talks in Abu Dhabi; the
COP31 leaders' summit opening in Antalya; Anthropic's Nasdaq IPO roadshow;
October US inflation and the December Fed hike it priced in; the House vote
shelving the Hegseth impeachment; the alleged Iran-backed plot against RAF
Fairford; the US Army officer arrested in Britain on suspicion of espionage
for Russia; El Salvador's strike on the 400 Mawozo gang's Haiti headquarters;
the ruling letting Trump's $10bn BBC defamation suit proceed; and Congo's
Ebola cases passing 11,000.

Every fact was checked against reporting published on 11 November 2026
(Reuters, AP, BBC, NPR, Bureau of Labor Statistics, Metropolitan Police,
Yonhap) before it was written.

## Files

- `summary.txt` — the summary (UTF-8, under 2,000 characters)
- `main.ts` — the entrypoint (Node 22, standard library only)
