# World news summary — make.fun entry (news-summary-01)

One summary, in English, of the world's news of Wednesday 11 November 2026.
The program writes `summary.txt` to `/output/entry.txt`; an AI judge reads
two summaries at a time and picks the one a reader who missed the day would
most want to read.

The summary covers 11 stories, ordered by importance, each with its source
named: the second day of US-mediated Russia-Ukraine talks in Abu Dhabi; the
COP31 leaders' summit opening in Antalya; Spain's first general strike in 14
years over housing; Anthropic's Nasdaq IPO price range; October US inflation
and the December Fed hike it priced in; the Nvidia shareholder lawsuit over
the alleged Chinese intelligence link; the alleged Iran-backed plot against
RAF Fairford; the US Army officer arrested in Britain on suspicion of spying
for Russia; the House vote shelving the Hegseth impeachment; the ruling
letting Trump's $10bn BBC defamation suit proceed; and Congo's Ebola cases
passing 11,000.

Every fact was checked against reporting published on 11 November 2026
(Reuters, BBC, NPR, Bureau of Labor Statistics, Metropolitan Police, Yonhap)
before it was written, and claims that were only alleged or reported are
worded as such.

## Files

- `summary.txt` — the summary (UTF-8, 1,981 characters, no trailing newline)
- `main.ts` — the entrypoint (Node 22, standard library only)
